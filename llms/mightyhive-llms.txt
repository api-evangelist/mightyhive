# MightyHive

> MightyHive was an independent data and digital media consultancy that helped brands take
> control of their programmatic advertising, media buying, data strategy and marketing
> technology. It was acquired by S4 Capital in 2018, merged with MediaMonks, rebranded to
> Media.Monks in August 2021, and now operates under the single "Monks" brand at monks.com.
> MightyHive is a professional-services business, not an API product company: it published
> no public API, developer portal, machine-readable specification, SDK or webhook surface,
> and the successor brand publishes none either.

## Status

- The MightyHive brand is retired. The operating company is Monks (S4 Capital plc).
- mightyhive.com still resolves but serves a frozen Drupal 8 archive of pre-2021 press
  releases, case studies and event pages. There is no developer section.
- No OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, Postman collection, MCP server or A2A agent card was
  found on any MightyHive or Monks host.
- No first-party client library exists on npm, PyPI, RubyGems or crates.io.

## Notes for agents

There is nothing here to call. Any task that requires programmatic access to MightyHive or
Monks media, campaign or measurement data must go through the underlying advertising
platforms (Google Marketing Platform / DV360, Amazon Ads, Meta and similar) that the
consultancy operates on a client's behalf, or through a direct commercial engagement with
Monks. Do not infer an API from the existence of this profile.
